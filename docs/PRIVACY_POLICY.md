# AR Coin Drive — Privacy Policy

_Published copy: Google Doc https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ROFQmTNnysuV_sqmUXvaxINBE8Jw5vGExDvVXngPVJA (the Google Doc is the source of truth)._

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

This policy explains what information the Android game **AR Coin Drive** ("the game", package `com.arcoindrive.game`), published by **TheLightCome** ("we"), uses and why. The game is intended for players aged 13 and over.

## 1. Information the game uses on your device

- **Camera.** In AR mode the game uses the camera, through Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), to detect floors, walls and obstacles so the virtual car can drive in your room. Camera images and depth data are processed on your device in real time. We do not record, store or send them anywhere. You can play in the virtual room without granting camera permission.
- **Motion sensors.** The gyroscope and accelerometer turn the view and enable tilt steering. This data stays on the device.
- **Game progress.** Coins, cars, upgrades, settings and your driver name are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
- **Local multiplayer.** When you host or join a game on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot, your phone exchanges your driver name, car choice and car position with the other phones in that game over the local network. Nothing is sent to our servers. We do not run any servers.

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information, and we do not have user accounts.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from **Google AdMob**: an optional rewarded video (watch an ad to double the coins from a round) and an occasional full-screen ad between races. Players with the VIP pass see no ads.

To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:
- the device's advertising ID,
- IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version),
- ad interactions (views, clicks) and crash/diagnostic data related to ads.

**Consent.** If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested, and you can choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings > Privacy > AD CHOICES**. Elsewhere you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy, https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## 3. Purchases (Google Play)

The VIP pass and the premium cars are one-time purchases made through **Google Play Billing**. Google processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of which items you own so it can unlock them and restore them on a new phone. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## 4. Game engine

The game is built with the **Unity** engine. Unity may receive basic technical information about the device (for example, the device model and OS version) for performance and crash diagnostics. See https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

## 5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We share no information ourselves. The only data that leaves your device is what Google (AdMob, Play Billing, ARCore) and Unity collect as described above.

## 6. Data retention and deletion

We store no data about you on any server. Game data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game. For data collected by Google, use the controls in your Google account and device settings described above.

## 7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected through the ad partners, contact us and we will help.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
